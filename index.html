Ontario Wind Load Calculator

SB-1 hourly wind pressures q by municipality, plus a simplified pressure calculation — OBC SB-1 Table 2.

Pick a municipality.

Scope

Hourly wind pressures q are read straight from SB-1 Table 2. The pressure p = Iw × q × Ce × (Cg × Cp) uses factors you supply from OBC 4.1.7 — this is a lookup and arithmetic aid, not a wind design. The same SB-1 row's snow values (Ss, Sr) are shown for reference.

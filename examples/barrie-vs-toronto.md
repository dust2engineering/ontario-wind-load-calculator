# Worked example 2 — Barrie vs Toronto

Barrie's snow load is far higher than Toronto's (Ss 2.5 vs 0.9 kPa) but its
wind pressures are lower: q(1/50) **0.36 kPa** vs Toronto's 0.44 kPa.
Port Credit, on the Lake Ontario shore, has the highest GTA q in the table:
0.48 kPa at 1/50.

Command: `python3 wind_load.py Barrie 50`
